📘 Full guide: how to use & publish
  1. Add account: tap ➕ NEW ACCOUNT.
  2. Upload: tap the green box and pick up to 100 screenshots (JPG/PNG/WEBP).
  3. Analyze: tap 🤖 ANALYZE after the Render backend is connected. Fix or add items by typing them.
  4. Contact: fill your name, number and link once in step 3. They are remembered for every account.
  5. Price: type the price, add an exchange line if you want, tap 🔄 Regenerate.
  6. Copy: tap 📋 COPY DESCRIPTION, then paste in WhatsApp/Telegram. Tap 💾 Save account to keep it.
  7. Publish this website: open the page in Claude, tap Share, choose who can open it and copy the link. Until you share, only you can see it.
  8. Use like an app: open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮ then Add to Home screen.
  9. Good to know: accounts and photos are saved in this browser on this device only, so they do not appear on other phones. AI analysis needs the Render backend URL configured by the site owner. Others can still type items manually.
